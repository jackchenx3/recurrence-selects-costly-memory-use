Immediate retrieval effects at recurrent and innovation targets in BIT/PL
Focused manuscript update for PHASE3-BIT-RETRIEVAL-CAUSALITY-002
Execution and estimates accepted at G8. Publication-review disposition is recorded in the accompanying release acceptance receipt.

MAIN RESULT

In the frozen BIT/PL late HALF-law checkpoint experiment, cached rather than paired-fresh retrieval produced material positive immediate effects on eligible-lineage survivor share and on the contribution of a hashed-selected focal eligible family at recurrent targets. At innovation targets, all three effects were bounded within their prespecified negligibility margins. Recurrent-target survivor accuracy increased, while its magnitude relative to the material and negligibility thresholds remained unresolved. These are one-turnover, identical-state effects in this implemented model and fixed initial-start mixture. They do not establish long-run mediation, an effect of intervening on recurrence, or a general mechanism.

DESIGN AND INFERENCE

The inferential unit was a block, not a survivor, candidate, target, arm or query. All 42,000 prespecified blocks were retained: 21,000 ALL_F and 21,000 ALL_M starts. Each checkpoint t was drawn uniformly from 193 through 256 before generating its factual history. At the cloned pre-target state, six complete one-turnover evaluations paired recurrent target T_(t-2) and innovation target I_t with CACHE, ALL_FRESH and FOCAL_FRESH retrieval. They shared the frozen update-t operator tape. Every block contributed to both target types; this pairing did not double the sample size. Only CACHE factual history generated the pre-checkpoint states; branch outputs did not feed forward.

The eligible set E comprised policy-M parents with a valid cache. For L and P, ALL_FRESH switched every eligible parent to fresh probes. For F, FOCAL_FRESH switched only one eligible parent in an all-CACHE background. The focal parent minimized the prespecified 64-bit hashed key, with lower slot breaking ties; the endpoint is defined under that hashed selection rule, not an exactly uniform idealization. The probability bound for any 32-key tie under the uniform-word idealization is 496/2^64. L and F are different interventions and are neither merged nor converted into per-carrier effects.

Every contrast below is CACHE minus FRESH. L is the eligible parents' descendant share among 32 survivors. P is total correct bits among the 32 survivors divided by 1,024. F is the focal family's descendant count divided by 32 and is unconditional, including blocks without an eligible focal parent. F was not divided by H or by the number of eligible parents.

Six prespecified bounded-mean Hoeffding intervals used exact rational arithmetic and frozen upward-rounded half-widths: 0.015608294208001326 for L/P and 0.002378115509733596 for F. Alpha was 0.012 for each L/P endpoint and 0.001 for each F endpoint, totaling 0.05. Under the prespecified independent-uniform-Philox-word working idealization, Bonferroni gives at least 95% simultaneous coverage of the six intervals. This idealization is not a mathematical or cryptographic proof of independent generator outputs. No new tests, fitted intervals, p-values, outcome-selected cohorts or postproduction record reanalysis were added.

At recurrent checkpoint targets, all eligible carriers' cached rather than paired-fresh retrieval for one turnover increased eligible-lineage survivor share by more than 1/64 (simultaneous interval 3.480912 to 6.602570 pp for CACHE minus FRESH; estimate 5.041741 pp).

It is sign-resolved, not classified as a material positive effect.

At innovation checkpoint targets, all eligible carriers' cached rather than paired-fresh retrieval changed normalized survivor accuracy by less than 1/32 in magnitude (estimate -0.571308 pp; interval -2.132137 to 0.989522 pp). This bounds, and does not establish absence of, an effect.

At recurrent checkpoint targets, the focal eligible parent's cached rather than paired-fresh retrieval for one turnover increased that family's contribution to the 32-survivor population by more than 1/256 (estimate 1.239360 pp; interval 1.001549 to 1.477172 pp for CACHE minus FRESH). This is the unconditional population-normalized focal-family endpoint, not a per-carrier or H-conditioned effect.

At innovation checkpoint targets, the focal eligible parent's cached rather than paired-fresh retrieval changed that family's population-normalized contribution by less than 1/128 in magnitude (estimate -0.194271 pp; interval -0.432082 to 0.043541 pp). This bounds, and does not establish absence of, an effect.

The three innovation point estimates are negative and their intervals include zero; these values are retained without treating bounded effects as absent. No endpoint received MATERIAL_NEGATIVE or NEGATIVE_MAGNITUDE_UNRESOLVED. That classification does not rule out adverse effects within the displayed bounds.

ELIGIBILITY AND STRUCTURAL LIMITS

H=1 occurred in 20,996/21,000 ALL_F blocks and 20,995/21,000 ALL_M blocks; H=0 occurred in nine blocks in total. Exactly 2,551/42,000 blocks had e=32. The complete 33-bin e histograms for each start, including zero and 32, are reported in ELIGIBILITY_DISTRIBUTION.tsv; every histogram sums to 21,000. All original H/e, factual-recurrence, start-specific numerator and factual-accuracy descriptives are retained verbatim in ALL_ORIGINAL_DESCRIPTIVES.txt.

The L structural bound is |L| <= min(1, e/8, (32-e)/8). L is forced to zero at e=0 and e=32, and F is zero at H=0. Thus bounded aggregate L effects, including the innovation label, must be read alongside the e distribution and this near-monomorphism constraint. No normalization by e or H and no additional e-stratified inference was performed.

IDENTIFICATION AND PROVENANCE LIMITS

Under the frozen operator, pairing compares retrieval interventions at the same stored state and operator tape. The claim averages over the endogenous late-window state distribution and the fixed equal ALL_F/ALL_M mixture. It does not extend to SHAM or ZERO-law states, TORUS, other models, equilibrium, invasion, evolutionary stability, allele effects, biological memory, displacement as an established mechanism, or mediation of previously published long-run enrichment. No novelty or priority claim is made. This reduced study does not complete PHASE3-SELECTION-PERFORMANCE-MECHANISM-001.

The audit replays factual history for blocks 0..63 only. For blocks 64..41,999, history provenance is supported by fixtures, the 64-block replay and check E, not by independent replay of every history. A forged but self-consistent pre-target state with consistently recomputed arms can pass checks C-J by design. Conditional replay establishes that arms follow from the stored state; it does not establish that the state is a valid history outcome. The accepted COND_FORGE demonstration makes this limitation explicit.

The protocol's prospective directional notes have no hypothesis, test or interpretive privilege. An eligible cache's selection-history relationship to T_(t-2) does not establish any endpoint's sign: probe quality, donor changes, interference, selected endogenous states and eligibility prevalence can all matter. No monotonicity theorem was supplied. The recorded intuition is not used to call these findings expected, implied or mechanistic confirmation.
