Figure 1. Immediate retrieval effects at paired checkpoint targets. All six prespecified means and simultaneous intervals come from the original EA-accepted 42,000-block results. Each block contributes to both recurrent and innovation targets; arms and targets are not replicates. Panels L/P compare CACHE with ALL_FRESH. F compares CACHE with FOCAL_FRESH in an all-CACHE background and remains unconditional, divided by 32 only, not by e or H. Units are percentage points on the separate normalized endpoints; L and F are neither merged nor per-carrier effects.

Dotted lines mark positive and negative material thresholds delta: 1.5625 pp for L/P and 0.390625 pp for F. These are not the negligibility margins; the latter are 3.125 pp and 0.78125 pp, respectively. The intervals cover jointly with at least 95% probability under the prespecified independent-uniform-Philox-word idealization; no generator-independence proof is claimed. Material, bounded and sign-only labels appear in Table 1 and SIX_ENDPOINTS.tsv. The innovation intervals include zero and do not establish absence of effects. The full history-provenance, near-monomorphism and hashed focal-selection limitations accompany the manuscript.

The figure is a display conversion of the original exact fractions. No new test, raw-record reanalysis or scientific program rerun was performed. Exact fractions and labels remain authoritative over rounded display values.
