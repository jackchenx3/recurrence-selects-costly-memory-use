Remaining scientific questions after manuscript integration
25 October 2026 — research preprint version 1.3.0

Decision

The accepted evidence supports a manuscript on costly-use-policy selection and immediate retrieval effects. No new computation is needed to report those narrow findings honestly. Stronger mechanism or generality claims remain unsupported. The items below distinguish a publication claim boundary from a proposed future experiment. This document authorizes no jobs, endpoints, cohorts, sweeps, retries or revival of the original incomplete Phase 3 protocol.

1. Long-run mediation of selective enrichment — essential only for a mechanism claim.
Studies 001-003 establish long-run arm contrasts. BIT002 intervenes on retrieval at a single stored state and resolves immediate recurrent lineage/focal effects. These do not identify how much of long-run allele enrichment is mediated by retrieval, survivor competition, policy-frequency feedback or changes in later states. No quantitative decomposition or evidence of mediation follows by comparing these separate estimands. Any future mediation claim needs a distinct, prospective intervention and estimand that handles state/policy feedback; retain the present descriptive connection meanwhile.

2. Target-effect heterogeneity versus changing recurrence — essential for a recurrence-intervention claim.
BIT002 evaluates recurrent and innovation targets at the same HALF-generated states. Its L/F target-effect differences are resolved; its P difference is unresolved. Neither identifies the effect of changing recurrence probability or lag on those states. A future environmental-law intervention must define its own distribution and question prospectively. The current result remains a checkpoint-target comparison under one aligned HALF law.

3. Generality of immediate retrieval effects — essential for a cross-bundle retrieval claim.
Study 003 reproduces a long-run classification in one alternative torus/tournament bundle. BIT002 has no TORUS retrieval-intervention arm. Cross-bundle long-run agreement cannot be relabeled replication of immediate retrieval effects. Only a separately scoped prospective experiment could support that extension; it is not a prerequisite for the present BIT/PL report.

4. Historical validity of all stored states — an explicit reproducibility limit.
BIT002 independently replays factual history for blocks 0-63 only. Other histories rely on fixtures, that replay and check E. A forged self-consistent state plus recomputed arms can pass conditional checks C-J. Current provenance does not provide independent full-history assurance. Preserve this limitation and the conditional scope of the causal interpretation; no postproduction producer/analyzer/auditor rerun is authorized to remove it. Any stronger assurance requires separately designed prospective validation or truly external reproduction.

5. Precision of recurrent population accuracy and the P target difference — unresolved, not an error to repair.
BIT002 recurrent P is positive but crosses both its material and negligibility thresholds; the recurrent-minus-innovation interval contains zero. Three negative innovation estimates have zero-containing intervals and lie inside their prespecified margins. Report those exact decisions. Do not add blocks, choose new margins, condition on eligibility or select a favorable derived result. A new precision question would need an independently planned new study, not a top-up of this cohort.

6. Retention, retrieval and per-carrier value — unsupported distinct questions.
The original allele couples retention and use. BIT002 changes retrieval at an existing state, leaving history and policy fixed; it does not isolate long-run selection on retention. Its F endpoint uses one hashed focal family, is unconditional and divided by 32; it is not a per-carrier or H-conditioned value. L and F use different treatment vectors. Retain these distinctions; do not convert their present estimates into another endpoint.

7. Eligibility and near-monomorphism — structural, not an omitted subgroup result.
L is forced to zero at e=0 and e=32, and F at H=0. The full distributions and exact bound accompany the results. They do not license post hoc e/H stratification, conditional ratios or exclusion of zero-effect blocks. A future conditional question requires a new prospectively justified estimand and inference plan.

8. External review and public dissemination — editorial steps, not new scientific evidence.
The original v1.2 and focused BIT002 addendum passed internal AI-assisted computational reviews. The integrated manuscript passed a separate nonauthor claim/content review and visual review; those reviews do not constitute external peer review. This version is publicly released on GitHub; no new Zenodo DOI, journal submission, author declaration or external acceptance is claimed. Decide the external venue and update package/version metadata after a concrete reviewed manuscript exists. Existing published records and original author information remain preserved; do not invent declarations or external status.

Recommended next scientific decision

Complete the integrated manuscript and obtain human scientific reading of its claim boundaries. If the desired paper is the narrow selection-plus-immediate-effects contribution, proceed to ordinary external review preparation without new simulations. If a long-run mechanism paper is required, select one essential question from items 1-3 and prospectively design it as separate work. A broad programme or automatic return to the original 46-estimate protocol is not justified by manuscript integration alone.
