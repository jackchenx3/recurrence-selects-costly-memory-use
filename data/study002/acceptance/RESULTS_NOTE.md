# PHASE2-PERFORMANCE-CONVERSION-002 result note

## Decision

The study is valid and scientifically complete. The frozen producer analysis finished without a validity failure, and the independent auditor found zero mismatches in the 64 selected replay blocks (384 paths, 98,304 update records and 12,582,912 candidate rows) and in the full 41,600-block record/estimate verification (249,600 paths, 63,897,600 update records and all 19 saved estimates).

The primary result is **bounded below the positive one-bit scale**. Directional cache information increased late-window population accuracy relative to the recurrence-gated, parent-distance-matched noninformative control by 0.864997 percentage points (familywise interval -0.652132 to 2.382125 points). The interval includes zero but lies wholly inside the prespecified ±3.125-point meaningful band. Both finite-horizon start contrasts also lie inside that band.

## Selection and performance separate

Directional information strongly changed allele selection even though it did not produce a resolved meaningful population-performance gain. INFO minus NONINFO memory-use frequency was 18.420305 points (16.968842 to 19.871767), and NONINFO itself remained 12.608275 points (11.882544 to 13.334006) above the exact neutral half-frequency. In contrast, INFO minus SHAM accuracy was 0.938124 points (-0.513339 to 2.389586), and NONINFO minus SHAM accuracy was 0.073127 points (-1.378336 to 1.524590); both were bounded below the positive one-bit scale with unresolved signs.

Thus, in this fixed finite-horizon model, directional cache information materially amplified selection on the supplied memory-use policy, yet the corresponding population-accuracy effect remained small enough to exclude a positive one-bit benefit. This sharpens the Study 001 conclusion rather than reversing it.

## Scope

`Delta_P` is the total effect of directional cache information beyond the parent and matched displacement length, mediated through evolved policy use. It is not a per-use value-of-information estimate. The shared permutation matches displacement length and one common coordinate relabeling but not every cross-genotype relation, and zero-weight displacements retain direction. The experiment tests one supplied cache, one HALF law deliberately aligned to its delay, one opportunity cost, one mutation rate, one horizon and one model class. It does not establish spontaneous memory origin, general population utility, equilibrium behavior, invasion, biological relevance or priority.

## Manuscript disposition

This is a substantive extension of the existing paper because it separates the informational content of retrieval from the cost of displacing a fresh proposal. It warrants one coherent revision of the existing manuscript, with the two experiments presented as a paired result. A separate paper or numerical extension is not warranted.
