# Research preprint v1.2.0 (2026-10-02)

Environmental recurrence selects a costly memory-use policy across two operator bundles without a resolved one-bit population benefit

This revision integrates the accepted post-v1.1 prospective Study 003 (PHASE2-TORUS-MEMORY-003), run in one independently implemented alternative operator bundle, into the existing paper. It adds:

- a revised manuscript and supplement (Part C), five figures with `FIGURE_DATA.json`, and revised figure, PDF and three-study statistics scripts;
- the Study 003 frozen design and design GO, Random123 provenance and the source, build, fixture and review records of the independent implementation;
- the Study 003 release producer source (one-line audit-header correction, not compiled or executed), the original producer source that generated the outputs, and the independently written auditor source;
- all 22 Study 003 saved estimate records, decisions, the analysis runner receipt, the acceptance record and the post-audit source-patch receipt;
- the outcome-blind production-output authentication and manifests, and selected compact production records;
- the audit history: the failed first audit attempt (original malformed headers), the 12-byte normalization receipt and derived-tree manifest, and the recovery audit receipts (zero mismatches);
- manuscript provenance: the v1.2 final seal, source authentication, build receipt, visual QA, formatting-only build failures and patch receipts, the 2,998-check statistics report, the QWEN-037 transport failure and the QWEN-037R MANUSCRIPT_GO review, receipt and acceptance.

Study 001 and Study 002 data and code are byte-identical to v1.1.0. Release records that v1.2.0 supersedes are kept under `provenance/history/v1.1.0/`. The title changed from "Environmental recurrence and directional cache information select a costly memory-use allele without a resolved one-bit population benefit" to the v1.2.0 title above.

Joint result: selection on a costly supplied-cache-use policy separated from any demonstrated population-performance benefit across two operator bundles at the declared scales. All adverse, unresolved and negative outcomes, the Study 002 infrastructure failure, the Study 003 audit-header failure and all claim limits are retained. The 63 saved estimate records form three separately accepted families. They are not independent studies or replications.

A separate Study 003 complete-outcomes archive, `phase2-torus-memory-003-complete-outcomes-v1.2.0.tar.zst` (possibly as numbered parts), accompanies this release. Zenodo archival publication pending. Manuscript/data: CC BY 4.0. Original code: MIT. Not externally peer reviewed.

# Research preprint v1.1.0 (2026-10-02)

Environmental recurrence and directional cache information select a costly memory-use allele without a resolved one-bit population benefit

This revision integrates the accepted post-v1.0 prospective Study 002 (PHASE2-PERFORMANCE-CONVERSION-002) into the existing paper. It adds:

- a revised manuscript and supplement (Part B, S16-S26), four figures with `FIGURE_DATA.json`, and revised figure, PDF and statistics scripts;
- the Study 002 frozen design, prospective execution-recovery addendum, authenticated producer and separately implemented auditor source, all 19 saved estimate records, decision, diagnostics, two internal audit receipts, accepted result note and acceptance record;
- the compact Study 002 production records (run manifest, preflight, and every `blocks.bin` and `paths.bin` from the 32 corrected-run shards);
- the prospective records: design GO; the failed original submission (authority, submission and failure, labelled infrastructure-only); the recovery review, addendum, authority and submission; and the production, analysis and audit acceptances;
- manuscript provenance: the v1.1 final seal, source authentication, build receipt, visual QA, provenance patch, the 1,975-check statistics report and the QWEN-019 MANUSCRIPT_GO claim audit.

Study 001 data and code are byte-identical to v1.0.0. The title changed from "Environmental recurrence selects a costly memory-use allele without a resolved one-bit population benefit" to the v1.1.0 title above.

Joint result: recurrence selected supplied-cache use and directional cache information further amplified that selection, while the total population-performance effects remained below the prespecified positive one-bit scale with unresolved signs. The adverse descriptive ZERO-law contrast, all unresolved and negative outcomes, and all claim limits are retained. The 41 saved estimate records form two separate families. They are not independent studies or replications.

# Research preprint v1.0.0

Environmental recurrence selects a costly memory-use allele without a resolved one-bit population benefit

This first release supplies the prospectively specified manuscript, supplement, three figures, authenticated producer/analyzer/auditor source, all 22 saved estimates, all block and path records, the bounded literature-search archive and reproducibility checks.
